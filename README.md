## 🔭 Research

My research focuses on **diffusion models**, **world models**, and **controllable image and video generation**.

- Efficient, structure-preserving image editing with diffusion and flow-based models.
- Identity-consistent video customization and sketch-guided video generation.
- Persistent geometric worlds that support continual scene expansion and consistent revisits.
